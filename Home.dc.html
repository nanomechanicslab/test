Professor, Department of Applied Mechanics
Indian Institute of Technology Madras

The Nanomechanics Lab explores how soft and responsive materials behave across scales from molecular interactions to nanoscale mechanics and macroscopic performance. We investigate polymer thin films, responsive soft matter, and emerging 4D materials by integrating nanomechanical experiments, molecular modelling, and multiscale approaches.
